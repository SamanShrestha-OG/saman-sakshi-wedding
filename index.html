Our Story

It all started during a season of breakups, bad timing, and very questionable decisions, especially from our best friends, who were very much in the middle of their breakup when they decided to play matchmaker. Emotional? Yes. Chaotic? Absolutely. Effective? Apparently.

What began as a simple escape for Sakshi turned into meeting the man who felt unexpectedly like home. For Saman, a chance meeting turned into something steady, grounding, and worth holding onto.

Across cities, chapters, and countless conversations, their paths kept finding their way back to each other. Curiosity turned into comfort, laughter turned into love, and what started as a coincidence slowly became a choice.

Two different stories. One perfectly timed, slightly messy meeting. And now, a lifetime of choosing each other, and forever reminding our friends that their breakup was, in fact, our meet-cute.
